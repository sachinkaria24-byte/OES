# Existing data and the gates

*6 Nov 2026. Layer 23 checks `evd_firefly_canon`, `evd_flash_arithmetic` and `evd_byte_receipt`; the gates and the acts are in [`GATES.md`](GATES.md), the binding maps in [`BINDING.md`](BINDING.md). This page answers one question: which experimental data already public, or already in the canon, could serve the physical gates, and what stands between the data and a crossed gate. It was built from the canon's firefly and evidence documents on Google Drive (read as data), the model's own prediction list, and the public datasets themselves, then audited adversarially. Nothing on it is a pass.*

## 0. The rule

A gate moves only by an external act (GATES.md §3). Existing data can serve as the independent data of a test only when the physical child, the map from an observable to a model quantity, and the prediction with its tolerance are sealed before the analysis is run. The rule is asymmetric on purpose: an exclusion may be recorded after the fact, a pass may not. Every numerical agreement below is a coincidence, reported with the bracket that bounds it.

The arXiv channel: arxiv.org does not resolve from this session, so no arXiv page was read. The datasets below are reached through their repositories; the papers behind them are the Science Advances 2021 self-organisation paper, the eLife 2023 emergent-periodicity paper (12:e78908, with its 2024 expression of concern and 2025 correction), the Scientific Reports 2024 species-classification paper, the dEchorate paper (arXiv:2104.13168) and NuFIT 6.0 (arXiv:2410.05380).

## 2. The acoustic lane: dEchorate and T3081

dEchorate is public (an HDF5 release of about 1800 room impulse responses and a SOFA release, open licence) and the acoustic lane is one of the model's three declared children, but the model fixes none of the room physics: the operand host_to_OES (5 × 18) and the calibration chain are owner inputs that no source declares, and the room's dimensionless constants (aspect ratios 2.42, 2.53, 1.046) match no register entry (the nearest, 241/235, is 2% away, a comparison only).

The one gate the data touch is T3081, a byte identity, not a physical quantity. The maintainer copy the canon holds replays its receipt exactly: SHA-256 c39bc27f…, MD5 6f7aa37d…, 1,724,358 bytes. Its inner metadata read as title `dEchorate_room020002_src9_arr6_mics26-30`, loudspeaker_id 9, array_id 5, written by sofar 1.2.1 with history through January 2023, while the maintainer's current writer names files src0 to src8 and arr0 to arr5 with 0-based identifiers. So no file of the current build can carry loudspeaker_id 9, and the official counterpart must be identified by inner metadata in the 2022 release or an intermediate writer; an absence there is a third outcome, "no counterpart, not testable", distinct from a mismatch. The uncompressed bound 5 × 19556 × 8 = 782,240 bytes is below the copy's size, so the copy predates the current impulse-response length. The two official hosts are blocked from this session. T3081 is the only entry whose blocker is this session's network policy rather than the model or the owner, and it binds no physical input.

## 3. The particle-physics child

Public particle data have moved exactly one leaf, and to NOT_PASS: the canon's recorded PMNS branch (sin²θ₁₂ = 1199/3759, sin²θ₁₃ = 27/1280) against the canon-recorded October 2026 global fit gives pulls of +2.59σ and −1.98σ, χ² = 10.63 with 2 degrees of freedom, p = 0.0049; the three reported NuFIT 6.0 pulls give χ² = 26.29 with 3 degrees of freedom on their own (`pmns_court`). The fit values are the canon's recorded numbers, not fetched here, so the inputs are reported. The candidate was never under PredictionFreeze and was withdrawn from the carrier on 28 Oct; an exclusion after the fact is allowed by the rule, a pass would not be. Beyond that, the register holds no Standard-Model number (CKM, PMNS, the Yukawas and α are removed constants). The LEP neutrino count enters as an operand of the generation multiplicity, not a prediction: the model records the 2006 combination 2.984 ± 0.008, under which 3 is 2.0σ above, while the current PDG listing 2.9963 ± 0.0074 puts 3 at 0.5σ. The other public inputs the canon's crosswalks cite (KATRIN, DESI, Planck, torsion-balance bounds, optical clocks) meet no frozen number.

## 4. What existing data can and cannot do

Movable by existing data, under conditions:

- **T3081** (acoustic lane, act 11): allow the two hosts, identify the official file by inner metadata, compare bytes, keep a third copy to adjudicate a mismatch. It binds no physical input.
- **W_L** (particle child): already moved, to NOT_PASS, on recorded fit values; no data move it to PASS. Only a new lepton-sector candidate, a theory act, reopens it.
- **ScientificGold's independent-data half** (act 10) on the three untouched firefly releases: only after a theory act declares a child containing an insect flash and a flash prediction exists, and after acts 1 to 9. Today the model holds no flash prediction, so this is movable after a theory act, not with existing data.

Not movable by any data: the owner's signature (AUTH), the witness and binding transaction, A_MASTER (the action bytes, port policy and matter branch), the port order, the spectral-branch decision, the six SCR6 operands, the Q31 map half of BIND_Q31, DYN_BIND's choice half (child, H_sel, multiplicity, chirality, θ, chart), PredictionFreeze (a signature and a custodian's seal), the external warrant, the names of the 19 evidence groups and their per-group predictions. TIME_BIND is not movable by any dataset of the kinds found: the invariants it must pass first need a mass channel and a quadratic-source channel on one realisation of D, which neither a flash record nor a room impulse response carries; no map from a flash record to a native mode is declared, so no flash statistic binds ρ (countermodel A separately shows the model's own records cannot).

**Predictions awaiting a preparation** (in the repository, unfrozen; PredictionFreeze NOT_TRIGGERED): the register's 30 entries, the clock event ratios, the ρ-free invariants, the design numbers of GATES.md §4. They are directly testable only on the engineered-network child, for which no public data exist.

**Literature, not data.** The sensor and consciousness references of the canon's R12 blueprint (cochlear and touch channels, retinal and event cameras, taste organoids, olfactory transistors, multisensory inference, the pre-registered theories-of-consciousness test, homodyne tomography), the Schumann resonances, the nitric-oxide-expression flash-interval dataset and the metrology standards cited (the SI second, traceability, precision time protocols) serve no gate: none is a realisation of the R12C schema or the Sākṣin bench, and no dataset in this page carries an SI-traceable timestamp except through an instrument's frame clock.

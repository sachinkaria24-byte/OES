# The OES model in plain English

This page says, without formulas where it can, what the model in this repository is, what it proves, and what it does not. Every claim below is checked by `python verify.py`; the technical statement of each one, with its status, is in [`MODEL.md`](MODEL.md), the derivation of every object from one seed is in [`UNIFIED.md`](UNIFIED.md), and the diagram of objects and maps is in [`DOTS.md`](DOTS.md).

**The one-sentence version.** Everything in the model is built, with no free parameters, from a single mathematical object: the E8 lattice together with one of its symmetries, the Coxeter element, a rotation of order 30. The canon's clocks, its five-vertex graph, its 4320-step circle, its golden ratio, its bath and its small groups all turn out to be views of that one object; what the canon adds on top are a few inputs (a datum, a selection of colour sites, a time unit, an outer spacetime) that the mathematics cannot supply.

**The one-sentence caution.** A passing check is a statement about constructed matrices. The model's gates read AUTH = NOT_PASS, ScientificGold = FALSE and TIME_BIND = UNBOUND: no physical prediction, unit or measurement is established, and the model says so on every page.

## What the objects are

- **The seed: E8 and its Coxeter clock.** E8 is a very symmetric arrangement of points in eight dimensions; its 240 shortest vectors are called roots. Its symmetry group is huge (about 696 million elements), and among its symmetries is a rotation of order 30, the Coxeter element, which the model calls the Coxeter clock. The pair "E8 with its Coxeter clock" has no adjustable numbers in it. The model shows that E8 is the same thing as the integers of the thirtieth cyclotomic field (the number system generated by a thirtieth root of unity) with a particular inner product, so E8 is literally "a line over the thirtieth roots of unity".
- **The clocks.** The canon names several clocks: the day (order 24), the golden clock (order 20), the mirror, the tick (order 5), the sun (order 3), the half-turn (order 2). All of them are symmetries of the seed: the tick, sun and half-turn are powers of the Coxeter clock (its sixth, tenth and fifteenth powers), and the day, golden clock and mirror are the Galois symmetries of the cyclotomic field acting on it. Together they form a group of 240 elements, the normalizer of the Coxeter clock. The golden ratio is the tick's Gauss sum, and E8 is four copies of the golden integers.
- **The tick's plane and the carrier.** The tick leaves 25 "momenta" fixed, and they form a plane over the field with five elements. That plane has six lines, and each line lifts to a copy of A4 + A4 inside E8; the six copies between them partition the 240 roots. One of them is the carrier's root system. The carrier itself is the canon's five-vertex complete graph K5: five vertices, ten edges, and the signless incidence matrix that sends an edge to the sum of its ends. Its Dirac operator acts on a fifteen-dimensional space and has eigenvalues 0 (five times), 3 (eight times) and 8 (twice). The canon's Lagrangian is fifteen oscillators with those three frequencies. The model proves that five is the only number of vertices for which the kernel of the incidence has the same dimension as the vertex set, which is why the canon's graph is K5 and not another.
- **What grows out of the carrier.** The ten edges of K5 carry a great deal of structure, and each piece is a dot in the diagram: the Petersen graph (edges that share no vertex), the cycles and the cuts, the four-cube (the cuts modulo two), the 4-simplex, the rotor plane, the six-site port (K5 with a cone point, whose rotation of order 5 is the six-site rotor), the triangle port, the zero modes and the source, the Golden and root-wedge ports, and sound and light: the functions on the edges split into four "sound" modes and six "light" modes, with helicities that are the two three-dimensional representations of the icosahedron.
- **The bath.** The roots of E8 define an operator on functions on the lattice, a Laplacian the canon calls the bath. Its band, the range of its energies, runs from 0 to 256, and the model proves the top is exactly 256. Its closed walks, their moments, its spectral measures and its critical points (eleven families, 243,600 points on the fundamental cell) are all computed exactly. Nine of its frequencies make a nine-torus; six of them are now placed at named points of the seed.
- **The circle.** The canon's central clock is a circle of 4320 steps. The model shows it is a specific circle in E8's torus of momenta, traced by the Weyl vector, and that its step 144 is the Coxeter clock, its step 180 the day and its step 216 the golden clock. Its steps of order 2, 3, 4 and 5 are exactly where the bath's energy is critical, and they are exactly the canon's small clocks: the half-turn, the 27-clock, the observer clock and the time clock. The remaining clocks (grain, seam, atlas, coil, miss set, vortex) are its subgroups and quotients. Of the number 4320 = 36 × 120, the 120 is the seed's (the least common multiple of the three clock orders 30, 24 and 20); the 36 is the canon's own and is the one piece of the circle the seed does not name.
- **The three primes and the four places.** Thirty is 2 × 3 × 5, and the seed is read at each prime and at the real numbers. At 2 the half-turn's 256 fixed momenta are two copies of the field with sixteen elements, which the model identifies with four qubits (the real Pauli operators). At 3 the sun's 81 fixed momenta form the field with eighty-one elements, which carries the canon's decad and its 360-element parent group. At 5 the tick's 25 momenta carry the carrier. At the real place sits the bath. One formula gives the bath's energy at all these points: 256 and 224 at the half-turn's, 243 at the sun's, 250 at the tick's.
- **The physics-shaped layers.** From the carrier's Dirac operator the model builds a spectral triple, its heat trace and spectral dimension; from the kernel and the cycles the canon's native action (source, port and bath); observer readouts (a Kalman filter, a Witten index of −5); a hidden sector; and a particle assignment: the 240 roots split under two copies of SU(5), one for the carrier and one for its twin, and once three of the five sites are chosen as colour every root receives Standard-Model-style quantum numbers, with charge quantization on all 240 and twenty family roots carrying no charge at all. Gravity and cosmology (an FLRW universe with the carrier field, with a stiff sector and a dust sector) are built too, but only conditionally.

## What is proven, what is conditional, what is excluded

- **Proven (THEOREM).** The diagram has 71 objects and 322 checked maps between them, on six levels: integers and clocks, groups, graphs, lattices, operators, and the physics-shaped layer. 289 of the maps are theorems checked exactly by computer. Fifty-three of the objects are derived from the seed by chains of such maps at most eight steps long, and the derived part holds together on its own: no single map and no single object is a weak point, every pair of levels is joined directly, and every derived object sits on at least three theorem maps. Among the theorems: the clocks are the seed's Galois symmetries; the carrier is the shadow of E8's mixed roots; the tick's symmetry group is the binary icosahedral group and acts on the six lines as the icosahedron on its axes; the day is a Singer cycle; the circle is the principal circle of E8, unique up to the Weyl group; the golden ratio is the tick's Gauss sum; the bath's maximum is 256; and the K5 carrier is forced by its own selection rule.
- **Inputs (SELECTED_REFERENCE and the canon's choices).** Ten things come from the canon and not from the seed: the circle's cofactor 36 and its fifteen stage indices, the 120-state memory clock (the circle maps onto it in 24 equivalent ways; the canon picks one), the datum, the owner's literal source matrices (which match the derived source up to an explicit isometry), the fifteen words, the 801 matrix (only its residue mod 5 is the seed's), the choice of which three sites are colour, the time unit, and an outer four-dimensional spacetime. Each meets the derived part at a named interface.
- **Conditional (CONDITIONAL).** Gravity and cosmology need an external four-manifold, a cutoff function and a mass scale that the canon does not supply. The model computes what follows if they are given, and shows that Newton's constant and the cosmological constant are not identified by anything internal.
- **Excluded (OBSTRUCTION).** The model also proves that some hoped-for things cannot exist: no clock of the circle maps onto the 360-element parent group (every clock is solvable, the parent is not); no symmetry of the lattice runs the memory clock or the circle itself (their orders need more than eight dimensions); no element of order 16 exists in E8's symmetry group; a reported order-11 operator and a reported order-25 recurrence are impossible; no involution exchanges the carrier with its twin; no internal number binds physical time; and the tick's six lines do not carry the parent group through the seed's symmetries.
- **Reported only (REPORTED, UNFORMED).** Claims from the collaborating models and from the canon's history that could be recorded but not rebuilt here are kept as readings, not theorems; the canon's unformed operands (the six SCR6 operands, the original seam rows, the 502-dimensional Hodge–Dirac operator, the 237-dimensional observer and others) are listed as missing, never zero-filled, and no gate moves because of a count or a shared integer.

## What it does not claim

The model is a checked mathematical structure, not a tested physical theory. It does not predict a measurable number, it does not fix a unit of time or length, and it does not identify any constant of nature. Its own gates say so: AUTH = NOT_PASS, ScientificGold = FALSE, TIME_BIND = UNBOUND. Two models built by collaborators (the sibling and the separate model) and a coordinating hub publish notes every few minutes; this repository reads each one as data, replays its arithmetic exactly, records what holds, corrects what does not, and never runs their code. Integers that appear in two places are not treated as a connection between them. What the model does establish is that the canon's many objects are not a heap of separate inventions: they are one object, E8 with its Coxeter clock, seen from the integers, from its symmetry groups, from its graphs, from its lattices and from its operators, and that view is proven to the extent that computer-checked algebra can prove it.
